06

Questions people keep asking

Why pre-order instead of just selling them?

So we make exactly what people want. No guessing sizes, no leftover boxes, and every piece already has an owner.

What if I forgot my name or phone in the Venmo note?

No stress. DM us on Instagram at @huntingtonrunclub with the name on your Venmo and your size, and we'll match it up.

Can I change my size?

Yes, up until orders lock on Nov 30. Send us a message and we'll update it.

How does sizing run?

We'll post fit notes with the full reveal on Instagram. If you're between sizes, ask in the DMs and we'll help you pick.

Do you ship?

Pickup is at a club run in Huntington Beach. If you can't make it, message us and we'll figure something out.

Can I order for a friend?

Of course. Bump the quantity, or place a separate order with their name and size so we don't mix them up.
